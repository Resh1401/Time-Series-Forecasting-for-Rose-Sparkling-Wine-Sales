1.	BACKGROUND

ABC Estate Wines is a renowned producer of a wide variety of wines. The historical sales data for the rose wine and sparkling wine variety, spanning the 20th century is obtained with the goal of boosting sales performance. By identifying emerging market opportunities and reinforcing our competitive advantage in the wine industry, these insights will inform strategic decision-making, optimize inventory management, enhance marketing efforts, and ultimately improve the company's sales strategies moving forward.
 
2.	OBJECTIVE

Our primary focus is to analyse the data using time series forecasting to:

* To explore the data, identify trends, patterns, and key factors that have influenced wine sales throughout the 20th century.
* By applying data analytics and forecasting methods, to derive actionable insights that will support strategic decision-making and optimize future sales strategies.
* To analyse and predict wine sales trends for the next 12 months using the historical data provided by ABC Estate Wines.
* To empower ABC Estate Wines with valuable insights and foresight, enabling them to improve sales performance, seize emerging market opportunities, and sustain a competitive advantage in the wine industry.

3.	ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

To increase rose wine sales based on the forecast provided by the model, several strategies can be implemented, taking into account the seasonal trends and growth forecast for 1996.

* Maximize Holiday Season Sales: Since the forecast indicates a peak in December with sales reaching 275 units, targeted promotional campaigns during the holiday season can be implemented. This could include special discounts or limited-time offers to increase demand during this period.
* Partnerships with Event Organizers: Partnering with event organizers, catering services or restaurants can promote the sales of rose wine during holiday events.
* Introduce Seasonal Promotions: During the off-peak months when sales dip below 25 units, creating off-season promotions or discounts to maintain steady sales throughout the year by considering bulk purchase discounts or loyalty programs to keep customers engaged.
* Seasonal Flavors: Introducing special seasonal flavors or limited-edition bottles to make the product more appealing during off-peak months can generate interest and attract customers to buy during typically low-sales months.
* Stock Management: Based on the forecast, ensuring that the inventory levels are aligned with the predicted seasonal variations by stocking more units ahead of the holiday season to meet higher demand and reducing stock during off-peak months could help.
* Expand Distribution Channels: Collaborating with more retailers, both online and offline, expands the availability of rose wine, especially during peak season.
* Global Expansion: If feasible, exploring international markets where rose wine is in demand, particularly in regions with growing middle-class consumers or places where holiday celebrations heavily feature wine would drive the sales.
